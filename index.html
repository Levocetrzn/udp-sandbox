💡 Why Does the Receiver Land on 0xFFFF?

          
The checksum is the bitwise inverse of the sum: Checksum = ~Sum.

          
Every 0 added to 1 gives 1. Therefore: Sum + (~Sum) = 0xFFFF (all 16 bits set to 1).

          

            Try a 4-bit demonstration:
            

              
              
              1011 + 0100 = 1111 (0xF)
